# DoubtDesk Design System: "The Study Desk"

## Concept
DoubtDesk is envisioned as the focused, tactile late-night study desk: translucent frosted glass panes resting above a warm, calming paper-mesh background that evokes illuminated notebooks, library study lamps, and collegiate slate. We pair the distinguished editorial warmth of Newsreader serif headings with the precision of IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, ensuring high-contrast legibility across every layer. Tactile glass surfaces, custom SVG vector signatures, and purposeful physical micro-interactions turn academic doubt-solving into a calm, rewarding ritual that feels handcrafted rather than generated by a prompt.

## 2. Signature Moments

1. **The Study Desk Hero Banner:**
   Personalized greeting with contextual time-of-day awareness, computed metrics badge row (real live counts only: open doubts, need reply, solved, study streak), and a direct high-visibility "Ask a doubt" trigger.

2. **The "Solved" Affirmation Transition:**
   Marking a question solved triggers a subtle emerald halo expansion, and the status label displays an inline SVG check that draws itself smoothly with a spring stroke. Confetti-free and mature.

3. **AI First-Answer Reveal:**
   Gemini first answers arrive within a distinctive parchment glass card with an "AI DRAFT" monospace badge, custom AI monogram glyph, and an explicit academic reassurance note ("Check with a senior or your teacher.").

4. **Omni-Search Command Palette (⌘K / Ctrl+K):**
   Full-screen blurred backdrop with floating search card allowing instant exploration across solved doubts, open threads, and classmate replies with keyboard escape handling.

5. **Handcrafted Vector Empty States:**
   When rooms or answer bank queries have no entries, bespoke SVG vector line art renders directly in code (study lamp, catalog card index, and compass) paired with actionable encouragement copy.

## 3. Component Hierarchy

- **Sidebar (Desktop) / Bottom Bar (Mobile):**
  - Persistent left navigation (228px) on viewport >= 768px.
  - Floating frosted bottom navigation bar on viewport < 768px with full touch targets (48px height) and visible labels.
- **Top Bar:**
  - Frosted glass strip with sticky pinning, section kicker, page title, global ⌘K trigger, and day/night theme toggle.
- **Doubt Thread Card:**
  - Layered reading surface with high opacity.
  - Top metadata: OPEN / SOLVED badge, student initial avatar with deterministic color, relative timestamp.
  - Main serif question with full word-wrapping protection.
  - Nested reply stack: classmate replies with colored initials, AI replies with distinctive monogram and badge.
  - Action footer: "Reply" toggle and "Mark solved" (exclusive to the original asker).
- **Composer:**
  - Multi-line textarea with character counter, contextual "Replying to doubt" pill, and disabled states during network transmission or disconnection.

## 4. Accessibility & Fallbacks

- **No Backdrop Filter Support (`@supports not (backdrop-filter: blur(1px))`):**
  Switches all glass surfaces to solid opaque `--paper-raised` with crisp solid borders.
- **Reduced Transparency (`@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)`):**
  Background blur is eliminated and panels render with 100% opacity solid fills.
- **High Contrast (`@media (prefers-contrast: more)`):**
  Borders thicken to 2px solid and contrast ratios exceed 14:1.
- **Reduced Motion (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`):**
  Mesh orb drift animation is suspended; transitions are set to instantaneous (`0ms`).
- **Keyboard Navigation:**
  Every interactive control features a 2px offset visible focus ring matching `--accent`.
